Who we are

Fixers, thinkers &
tech collaborators.

Led by a dedicated squad of support technicians, we are DigiDesk—the school's core IT Support Office (ITSO) where you get to resolve terminal network crashes, software bugs, and account issues directly with the builders.

We pride ourselves on our systematic troubleshooting process, our quick turnover response speed, and our precise attention to detail. We know how stressful locked accounts or failing system access can be right before major school hours. That is why we maintain transparency through a public live ticketing log system.

We remain agile, highly distributed, and fully adaptive to campus emergencies. There's no bureaucratic middleman to navigate here, and no waiting weeks on an email thread—just five technical problem solvers who are assigned straight to your specific problem category.
